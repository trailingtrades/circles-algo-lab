Tier 1 · Basic · Week 1 · Market, maths and your AI co-pilot · Day 3 · S03

Make AI your trading co-pilot (Level 0)

AI Ko Trading Co-Pilot Banao (Level 0)

Concept: Verify before trustAI Lab: Three starter promptsPsychology: Automation bias90 min

Video abhi attach nahi hua

Class recording (YouTube unlisted) admin editor se jodi jayegi. Tab tak Read aur AI Lab se shuru kijiye.

01

AI kya kar sakta hai, kya nahi

Claude ya koi bhi LLM text ko samajhne, summarise karne, structure dene, aur checklist banane mein bahut achha hai. Woh future price nahi jaanta, live data nahi dekhta (jab tak aap paste na karo), aur confident galat bhi bol sakta hai. Isliye is course ka rule: AI = analyst, human = trigger. Decision hamesha aapka.

02

Hallucination: confident jhooth

Hallucination tab hota hai jab AI aisi cheez bataye jo sach lagti hai lekin hai nahi: galat P/E, jhoothi news, na-hone wala circular. Bachne ka tareeka: AI ko sirf woh data do jo aapne verify kiya hai, aur usse bolo ki jo nahi pata woh 'missing' likhe, guess na kare.

03

Prompt structure: context, task, format

Achha prompt teen hisson ka hota hai. Context: aap kaun ho, kya data hai. Task: exactly kya chahiye. Format: output kaise chahiye (table, 5 bullets, JSON). 'Is stock ka kya lagta hai' bura prompt hai. 'Yeh press release paste kar raha hoon, 3 facts, 2 guesses, 1 risk nikalo' achha prompt hai.

04

Verify-before-trust routine

Har AI output par teen sawal: (1) Yeh claim kahan se aaya, source hai? (2) Kya main ise 30 second mein khud check kar sakta hoon? (3) Agar yeh galat hua to mera kya nuksaan? Jo claim in teen se nahi guzarta, woh decision mein nahi jaata.

05

AI-usage disclosure

SEBI ke Research Analyst rules mein AI ke use ka disclosure zaroori hai. 5 Circles ka approved wording Compliance Officer se sign-off ke baad har material par aayega. Aapke liye seedha rule: jab bhi aap kisi ko AI-generated analysis bhejo, saaf likho ki AI ne banaya hai aur aapne verify kiya hai ya nahi.

Outcome: Aap AI ko blindly follow nahi, verify karke use karoge.
